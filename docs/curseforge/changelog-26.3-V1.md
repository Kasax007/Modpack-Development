## Dreamcraft 26.3 V1 - Dreamcraft on the newest Minecraft

**Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.20.1, Java 25.** Based on Dreamcraft 1.20.1 V4: every mod that exists for 26.3 came along, the rest was replaced by new mods for the same job.

### New
- **A new quest story** in 13 chapters and over 160 quests (Prologue, The Settler, Into the Deep, Sparks of Industry, Paths of Power, Through the Fire, The Endless Sea, The End of the Dream, Epilogue and four side paths). Every quest explains how its mod really works. Press **J** to open the book.
- Quests run on **Quest Icons 1.0** (FTB Quests is not available for 26.3): quest map, chapters, tasks, rewards, loot crates, teams, pins and an in-game editor.
- **Beach Craft (Raft) 2.0** - the raft ocean with sharks, purifiers, grills, crop plots and lots of new decoration.
- New: **RPG Series** (Wizards, Paladins & Priests, Archers, Rogues & Warriors, Jewelry, Relics, Arsenal, Armory), **Tech Reborn**, **Quarry Reborn**, **Mutant Monsters**, **Woodwalkers**, **Tectonic**, **Towns and Towers**, **Dungeons and Taverns**, **ATi Structures**, Moog's structures, **Farmer's Delight** and its add-ons, **Cooking for Blockheads**, all **Macaw's** mods, **Rechiseled**, **Dawn of Time** and many more.
- New main menu and loading screen in the Dreamcraft style, with eight backgrounds.
- **JEI** with Just Enough Professions and Just Enough Resources for recipes.
- **Server pack** with start scripts for Linux, macOS and Windows.

### Tested
- World creation, the overworld, Nether, End and the raft ocean, on the client and on a dedicated server
- Chunk pre-generation with Chunky in all dimensions without errors

### Not in this version
Mods that are not available for 26.3 yet (for example Create, Botania, Applied Energistics 2, Spectrum, Ad Astra, Modern Industrialization and The Bumblezone) or that break the game on 26.3 are not included. They come back when they are ready.
