## Dreamcraft 26.3 V1.1 - Beach Craft 2.0.1 and quest fixes

A bugfix update. Every feature of Beach Craft was filmed for a review, and everything that did not work as it should was fixed.

### Updated
- **Beach Craft (Raft) 2.0.1**:
  - Sharks spawn in the raft ocean again.
  - Sail canvas can be crafted again.
  - The paddle pushes the boat.
  - The item catcher, water purifier, grill and scarecrow are fixed.
  - Driftwood and palm wood burn in furnaces.
  - Quarries keep shulker box contents.
  - Batteries no longer bounce energy back and forth.
  - Portal and teleporter fixes, and more.
  - The full list is in the Beach Craft changelog.

### Quests
- **The Endless Sea** chapter matches the mod again:
  - Driftwood grows as stumps on sandy beaches.
  - The receiver leads to floating driftwood.
  - The old "Land Ho!" quest asked for a structure that was almost impossible to find. It is now **Driftwood Ahoy!**: bring 16 driftwood logs aboard.
  - The titanium quest names the diamond pickaxe and the metal detector.
  - The titanium armor quest lists the real set bonus: Water Breathing, Dolphin's Grace and Conduit Power.

### Server
- The server pack contains Beach Craft 2.0.1 and the new quest files.
- To update an existing server, replace `mods/raft-*.jar` and `config/questsicons/quests`.
