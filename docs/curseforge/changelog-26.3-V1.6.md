## Dreamcraft 26.3 V1.6 - Beach Craft 3.4

### Beach Craft 3.4.0
- **Watch the cooking pot cook**: the ingredients stir in the pot, the water takes the colour of the dish and a chime
  rings when it is ready. The pot only takes what fits a dish and tells you what is still missing.
- **Driftwood Fishing Rod** with its own tackle box: load Fish Bait and Golden Lures, every cast uses one of each.
- **The Endless Sea**: the big places are much rarer and lie further apart, they stand on the ground, and many of
  their barrels and crates hold loot now. With Lootr (in Dreamcraft) every player gets their own loot there too.
- **JEI pages** for the cooking pot, the fish trap and the island crops.
- New looks for the banana and all dishes, a three-block-tall parasol you can stand under, fixed rope rug, lantern,
  planter, flower box and vase, still water around raft foundations, and sorted creative tabs.

### Quests
- Island Life: the Golden Lure quest is now **Tackle Box** (craft the Driftwood Fishing Rod and load it). The cooking
  pot quest explains the new pot.

Your worlds and quest progress are kept. The new distances, footings and barrel loot of Beach Craft show up in parts
of the Endless Sea you have not explored yet.

### Server
- The server pack contains Beach Craft 3.4.0 and the new quest files.
- To update an existing server: replace mods/raft-*.jar and the folder config/questsicons/quests.
