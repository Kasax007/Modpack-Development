## Dreamcraft 26.3 V1.3 - Beach Craft 3.0, Tamagotchi and the Dream Star

The biggest Dreamcraft update since the move to 26.3: a whole new sea, ten pets to befriend, an endgame trophy and
twice as many quests.

### Beach Craft 3.0 "Tides of the Deep"
My raft mod got its biggest update so far. The Endless Sea is no longer one empty ocean:
- **An archipelago** of sandy islands, coral reefs, kelp forests, drowned shelves and dark trenches, in **12 biomes**:
  palm, jungle, mangrove and volcanic isles, glowing lagoons, frost floes with icebergs, wreck graveyards and more.
- **A story.** You arrive as a castaway, and a chest with the **Expedition Journal** is waiting for you. Logbooks,
  research terminals and crackling radios lead you from an old lighthouse to a village on stilts, a research station
  on the sea floor, a drowned city, a forge in a volcano and a temple on the reef. What waits at the very bottom of the
  sea? Find out for yourself...
- **The Expedition Raft**: four seats, storage, and upgrades you can see: sail, engine, anchor, cargo crate and lanterns.
- **Diving**: helmet, oxygen tanks, wetsuit, flippers, a helmet lamp for the dark deep, an air compressor, a harpoon
  gun and a sea scooter.
- **12 new creatures**: whales, manta rays, jellyfish, sea otters, crabs, penguins, seagulls, moray eels, anglerfish,
  hammerhead sharks, ember crabs and drowned corsairs. Bruce the shark now bites your raft!
- **Places to explore**: galleon and freighter wrecks, sunken planes, rusted rigs, iceberg outposts, castaway camps and
  a hidden pirate cove.
- **Hundreds of new blocks**: weathered and teak wood, coral stone, tidestone, sea glass, ship hulls, catwalks, paper
  lanterns, string lights, a ship cannon, treasure chests, a hammock and much more.
- **The Seafarer's Almanac**, an in-game guide book for everything in Beach Craft.

### New mod: Tamagotchi 1.2.0
- Ten rare pets live in the world's structures: three dragons, the Frost Penguin, the Ember Pup, the Sculk Wisp, the
  Void Kitten, the Pebble Golem, the Hex Toad and the Mimic.
- Tame one with a Tamagotchi Heart and look after it: feed it, play with it, keep it clean and let it sleep. A happy
  pet follows you, carries a backpack, fights at your side, brings you golden apples when you are hurt and uses its
  talent - from smelting your ore to sniffing out diamonds.
- Toys, treats, beds, a pet bath, plushies and the Tama Pager. The Tamacon book explains everything.

### Quest Icons 1.1.0 and the Dream Star
- **The Dream Star** is the endgame trophy of Dreamcraft, like the ATM Star of All The Mods. Its recipe needs a
  treasure from every corner of the pack: 2 Ametrine Ingots, a Quest Crown, a netherite staff, a Nether Star, a
  Lapotronic Energy Orb, a Tamagotchi Heart, a Dragon Egg or Dragon Head and the Heart of the Tide.
- Put it on display on the **Dream Star Pedestal**: it floats, turns and sparkles. Crafting it unlocks the challenge
  "Living the Dream".
- Quest book fixes: Escape closes the open quest first, buttons scrolled out of view no longer react to clicks, and
  a player could land in an empty team after a server restart. The full list is in the Quest Icons changelog.

### Quests: 164 -> 328
- **6. The Endless Sea** is written new for Beach Craft 3.0 (81 quests): the portal, the almanac and the journal,
  raft survival, the whole story as quests that follow its chapters (without spoilers), and side quests for sailing,
  diving, creatures, wrecks, building and the sea's own technology.
- **New side path: Tamagotchi** (27 quests): where every pet lives, taming, care, toys, the backpack, talents,
  furniture and the hunt for all ten.
- **The Dream Star** in the Epilogue: after **Master of Dreams**, one quest per ingredient tells you where it comes
  from, then you craft the star and put it on its pedestal. Master of Dreams now gives **two Quest Crowns**, because
  the star uses one up. Already finished it? Open the quest: the second crown is waiting for you.
- **Every chapter shows more of the pack**:
  - 3. Sparks of Industry: the way to the **Lapotronic Energy Orb** (advanced circuits, assembling machine, chemical
    reactor, multiblock machines, iridium and the implosion compressor), Quarry Reborn, Tesseract, Dark Utilities,
    trash cans and power tools.
  - 4. Paths of Power: netherite staves, spell books, Relics and the epic armor of Armory.
  - 1, 2, 5 and 7: Carry On, Healing Campfire, More Villagers, the watering can and chicken nest, Necoium, Easy
    Anvils, the crafting terminal, nether rubies and nagas, a nether city, BetterEnd's metals and eternal portals,
    the dragon egg and head, shulker boxes, and where to find the Ember Pup and the Void Kitten.
  - Side paths: rice, coffee, maple syrup and a whole kitchen; Stoneworks, Macaw's furniture, Magic Vibe
    Decorations and hanging chains; sky islands, castles, pillager outposts, trial chambers and Lost Relics;
    safari animals, golems, snails, Woodwalkers, the whole mutant family and the Wildfire.
- Loot crates can now also hold pet kibble, rope, titanium, Tamagotchi Hearts, ancient crystals and netherite
  upgrade templates.

### Fixed
- "Let the Machines Dig" named the wrong mod: its quarry is **Quarry Plus** from Additional Enchanted Miner.
  **Quarry Reborn** has its own quest now.
- The Mythic Upgrades quest told the wrong places for its gems. Ametrine comes from the End.
- Quests call the Tech Reborn battery by its real name, Battery Box. The Explorer's Atlas no longer names Wilder Wild,
  which is not in the pack.
- Two rewards named items that do not exist in Minecraft 26.3, so they gave nothing: "Path of the Wizard" now gives an
  Arcane Wand, "Chalk Marks" a Glowing Chalk.
- Two quests wrote "Friendsoes" instead of Friends&Foes (the "&F" was read as a colour code).
- A few quests sat right on the line between two other quests; the maps are tidied up.

### Server
- The server pack contains Beach Craft 3.0.0, Quest Icons 1.1.0, Tamagotchi 1.2.0 and the new quest files.
- To update an existing server, replace `mods/raft-*.jar` and `mods/QuestIcons-*.jar`, add `mods/tamagotchi-*.jar`
  and replace `config/questsicons/quests` - or use the new server pack.
- **Existing worlds:** parts of the Endless Sea that you have already explored keep their old shape. Sail farther out
  to find the new islands and story places. Your old arrival platform has no castaway chest: craft an Expedition
  Journal (book, compass and paper) and sneak-use it at sea for a chart to the first story place.
- Quest progress is kept. Quests of the old Endless Sea chapter that still exist keep their progress.
