## Dreamcraft 26.3 V1.6.1 - Mod update

Mod update: Beach Craft 3.4.3, Quest Icons 1.3.1 and Tamacraft 1.3.1.

### Beach Craft 3.4.3 (the pack had 3.4.0)
- **Machines keep their energy**: break a Makeshift Battery, Makeshift Solar Panel, Quarry, Chunk Destroyer, Wind
  Turbine or Air Compressor and its item keeps the energy that was in it.
- **Item Catcher**: nine slots for the catch instead of one, so a second kind of debris no longer stops it.
- **Chunk Destroyer**: only a column in which it really mines something costs energy.
- **Plastic Hook**: it no longer pulls items through walls, floors or glass.
- **Driftwood is no tree**: no more Driftwood Saplings from drifting barrels or driftwood leaves (the leaves drop
  sticks now). Saplings already in your world stay.
- JEI shows readable names for all item tags of Beach Craft ("Grill Fuel", "Island Seeds", "Fishing Bait").
- Giant Clam: a closed clam or one without a pearl shows a proper message.

### Quest Icons 1.3.1 and Tamacraft 1.3.1
- JEI shows readable names for the item tags of Quest Icons.
- The links of Tamacraft and Beach Craft in the mod list lead to the right pages.

### Pack
- The game window is named "Dreamcraft 26.3". It carried the name of another pack.

Your worlds and quest progress are kept.

### Server
- The server pack contains Beach Craft 3.4.3, Quest Icons 1.3.1 and Tamacraft 1.3.1.
- To update an existing server: replace mods/raft-*.jar, mods/QuestIcons-*.jar and mods/tamacraft-*.jar.
