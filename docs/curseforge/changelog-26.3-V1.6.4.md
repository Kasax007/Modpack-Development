## Dreamcraft 26.3 V1.6.4 - Choose your recipe

- **New mod: Polymorph+.** Some mods add recipes with the same ingredients as other mods, so you always got the same
  one of the results. Four copper ingots, for example, make a Copper Trapdoor or Dawn of Time's Copper Roof Tiles, and
  three string make Beautify's rope or Beach Craft's rope. Now a small button appears above the result slot when that
  happens: click it and pick the item you want. It works in the crafting table, the furnaces, the smithing table and
  the crafter, and your choice stays as long as the ingredients stay the same.
- Mod update: Quest Icons 1.3.3, Beach Craft 3.4.5 and Tamacraft 1.3.3.
  - Beach Craft: the Cutlass had the same recipe as the Machete. It now takes three iron ingots.
  - Tamacraft: wheat with a cod or a salmon made both Fish Crackers and Pet Kibble. Fish Crackers now take two wheat.

Your worlds and quest progress are kept.

### Server
- The server pack contains Polymorph+ and the updated mods. Server and players need V1.6.4, because Polymorph+ runs on
  both sides.
- To update an existing server: replace mods/QuestIcons-*.jar, mods/raft-*.jar and mods/tamacraft-*.jar and add
  mods/polymorph_plus-fabric-1.5.0-26.3.0.jar and config/cardinal-components-api.properties, all from the server pack.
  Then restart the server.
