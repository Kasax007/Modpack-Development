## Dreamcraft 26.3 V1.5 - Island life, Tamacraft and a quest editor

### Beach Craft 3.3.0 (with everything from 3.2.0)
- **Sail your own raft for real**: stack a **Mast**, join **Sails** into one big sheet and furl them, drop the anchor
  with an **Anchor Winch**, put fuel into the **Raft Motor** through its own screen, and watch the wheel turn as you
  steer. Sneak and use the wheel to moor (the raft becomes blocks again). Setting sail no longer stalls.
- **A wider, calmer Endless Sea**: more open water, mostly palm beach islands, about 60% fewer structures that never
  grow into each other, richer chests, seagull flocks in the sky and much less flotsam. The Item Catcher now catches
  while your raft sails.
- **Islands full of life**: turtles, parrots, rabbits, chickens, pigs, reef fish and foxes.
- **Guarded places**: most places are guarded now, and the guards get tougher the further the story leads you.
  New enemies: scavengers, reef guardians and corsair crews, led by the Scavenger Chief, the Corsair Captain and the
  Reef Warden.
- **The Leviathan** keeps to the deep of its pool and fights harder (750 health, angrier Tidelings). Every fight takes
  a new Tidewarden Key, and the rewards land next to you.
- **Cozy living**: 44 new blocks - benches, loungers, tables, serving plates, shelves, baskets, lanterns you can switch,
  a steel drum and more. Sit down near cozy things and you are **Rested**: you heal and get hungry slower.
- **The island kitchen**: sweet potatoes, chili, bananas, rice and sea grapes, a Cooking Pot with three soups and
  curries, a Fish Trap and 15 new dishes.
- **Gear**: a boomerang that fetches floating loot, a machete, a shark tooth spear, shell armour, an explorer's outfit,
  a wreck compass and a captain's spyglass.
- Cleaner block models (no ragged edges or flickering faces) and a new mod icon.

### Tamagotchi is now Tamacraft (1.3.0)
- Same mod, new name. Your pets, items and furniture carry over: worlds are moved to the new names when they load.
- The Tamagotchi Heart is now the **Tamacraft Heart** (the old one still works in the Dream Star recipe).

### Quest Icons 1.3.0
- **470 quest icons** (letters, numbers, arrows, tiers, medals and chapter themes) and 56 pictures for the quest map.
- **Edit the quest book in the game**, no SNBT needed: click Edit in the book (operators) and build quests with forms
  and pickers.

### Quests (369)
- **New side path "Island Life"** (21 quests): guarded places and their captains, sail and anchor winch, cozy living
  and the Rested effect, island crops, the Cooking Pot and the Fish Trap, the boomerang, shell armour, the explorer's
  outfit, the wreck compass and the golden lure.
- **The Endless Sea**: the Leviathan, the Plastic Hook, the Item Catcher and your own raft explain how they work since
  Beach Craft 3.2.
- Tamagotchi is called Tamacraft in every quest (the pets path, the Dream Star and the crates).

Your worlds and quest progress are kept. Beach Craft's new sea, its animals and its wild crops show up in parts of the
Endless Sea you have not explored yet.

### Server
- The server pack contains Beach Craft 3.3.0, Tamacraft 1.3.0, Quest Icons 1.3.0 and the new quest files.
- To update an existing server: replace mods/raft-*.jar and mods/QuestIcons-*.jar, replace mods/tamagotchi-*.jar with
  mods/tamacraft-*.jar, and replace config/questsicons/quests.
