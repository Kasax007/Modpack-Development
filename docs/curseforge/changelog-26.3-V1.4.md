## Dreamcraft 26.3 V1.4 - Build your own raft, the people of the sea, and bigger chests

### Beach Craft 3.1.0
- **Build your own raft**, block by block, like in Raft. Place Raft Foundations on open water and build on them:
  floors, walls, chests, furnaces, crops and redstone. Set a **Helm** and sail away. Everything keeps working at
  sea: furnaces smelt, crops grow, chests open. Oars, sails and the Raft Motor give your raft power. Drop anchor and
  the raft becomes blocks again, just as it was.
- **People of the Sea**: eight people live in the old places of the Endless Sea. Talk to them, ask for a hint for your
  next step in the story, get a welcome gift and trade for pirate gold and pearls.
- **Sounds and music**: every creature and machine has its own sounds, and the sea has its own music by day and by
  night, for its places and for the fight against the Leviathan (music by Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0). The Raft Motor
  hums louder the faster you sail.
- **Creatures**: seagull flocks fly in a loose V in long curves, moray eels wait level in their holes and lunge,
  and crabs visibly dig themselves in when you sprint at them.
- **Day and night** in the Endless Sea, structures that fit into the islands, the Coral Temple always under water,
  six new island plants, and a Sea Scooter that runs three times as long.
- **Places stay whole**: basalt, ash, lava, trees and ore veins no longer grow into structures (the rooms of the Ashen
  Forge stay free), places no longer overlap (no Rusted Rig on the Abyssal Sanctum), and tiki bars and castaway camps
  only stand on flat ground. Checked block by block at 95 places in two worlds.

### New mod: Upgraded Iron Chests
- Iron, Gold, Diamond and Netherite Chests with much more room. They are immune to fire and lava.
- Safe Chests with a passcode, a search field in every upgraded chest, Quick Stack and Dump to nearby chests.

### Quests (348)
- **New side path "Quality of Life"** (16 quests): the upgraded chests, the safe chest, search and quick stack, drawer
  upgrades, sorting, Crafting Tweaks, shulker boxes, easy harvest, the minimap, lights, AppleSkin and zoom.
- **The Endless Sea**: new quests for your own raft (helm and the Shipwright advancement, sail and motor) and for the
  people of the sea (A Friendly Face, Every Soul Aboard). Raft Lanterns is no longer optional: the raft-building path
  follows it.
- The Mutant Zombie quest explains that it gets back up after it falls: kill it while it burns, or knock it down again
  until it stays down.
- Every quest of the book was completed in the game by a test run before this release.

### Server
- The server pack contains Beach Craft 3.1.0, Upgraded Iron Chests and the new quest files.
- To update an existing server: replace mods/raft-*.jar, add mods/upgraded-iron-chests-*.jar and replace
  config/questsicons/quests.
